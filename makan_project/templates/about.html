About MAKAN

Blending AI-powered design with professional engineering expertise.

Our Story and Vision

MAKAN was founded on the belief that cutting-edge technology and human creativity should converge to revolutionize architecture and interior design.

We saw a gap between high-cost, slow traditional design processes and the potential speed and innovation offered by **Artificial Intelligence**. Our platform bridges this, offering clients rapid, tailored, and cost-effective design solutions.

Our Mission:
  • To make professional design services accessible to everyone.
  • To empower engineers with advanced AI tools.
  • To deliver stunning, functional, and sustainable spaces.
